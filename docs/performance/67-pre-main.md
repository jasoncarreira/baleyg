## PR #99 round3 transient publication contention repair (exact-head review and both CI runs pending)

- **Fail-before-fix on pushed `b83174e`:** browser Q1 finished, then accepted CLI Q2's rollback-journal SQLite publication COMMIT returned `storage_busy`; push CI showed durable Q2 `failed/index_failed`. Deterministic pre-COMMIT injection reproduced false terminal failure in both CLI drain and daemon takeover before the scoped repair. Green PR-triggered CI on that old head did not waive red push CI.
- **Same ACK and FIFO:** the publisher records the authenticated old publication pin at admission. On typed SQLite `Busy`/`Locked` or exact SQLite lock-contention `storage_busy`, a verified owner re-reads the exact claim, seq, current root and durable pin. Only an unchanged pin and still-running same claim permit guarded same-seq requeue; the next tick can recapture and finish the original accepted ACK. The fast-path/cached completion retry handles terminal queue-write contention without repeating a successful publication. Broad invariant messages such as `storage_busy: cached claim changed` are nonterminal but not blanket-retryable; a stale owner cannot requeue or finish its successor's claim.
- **Ambiguous COMMIT and fences:** a changed pin after an apparent COMMIT error leaves the row running for fresh verified reconciliation, never reports false success or requeues an already published pin. Ctrl-C still leaves accepted work unfinished for a new verified owner; root/claim/pin fences, retained publication pins, and observational existing-only Status remain unchanged. A daemon tick drops an invalid holder and verifies a successor rather than spinning forever on the wrong incarnation. Best-effort bounded daemon diagnostics cannot panic or stop queue progress when stderr closes.
- **Real-process probe:** a separate SQLite RESERVED writer holds the live index after an accepted browser POST. The original durable job remains nonterminal after capture/attestation and same-seq deferral; native/graph pair and prior pin stay unchanged while public Status, source and packet refuse stale success. After the writer releases, the **same original job id**, without a second POST, finishes `done` at the next verified pin with paired normalized evidence and graph bytes. Focused post-COMMIT ambiguity, stale-claim, broken-stderr, and T04 tests plus all-target gates passed.
